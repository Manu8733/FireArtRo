# Night Runway - Storyboard

## Global Transition Grammar

1. **Ignition:** a thin blue line reveals the next section.
2. **Acceleration:** media moves on a slower z-plane than copy.
3. **Lock-on:** headings settle before controls become interactive.
4. **Afterglow:** sections leave a restrained light trail as they exit.

## Landing

1. Existing video remains full bleed; hero composition moves approximately `6vh` upward on desktop.
2. A short black brand statement bridges the hero and the performance stage.
3. Services share one pinned full-width scene; media and copy change in place, never as cards.
4. The stage remains video-led, with no decorative canvas competing with the footage.
5. A blue-black shutter reveals a compact selected-work viewer, a four-step line and the final CTA.

## Packages

1. A compact heading introduces the decision in one sentence.
2. Categories form a vertical editorial index beside one active visual.
3. Variants, facts and CTA update in place behind one-color blue-black bands.
4. No package is presented as an isolated pricing card.

## Gallery

1. A compact page heading leaves the media dominant.
2. Filters form a numbered editorial rail.
3. One medium projection and a narrow filmstrip control the viewing rhythm.
4. Opening a project uses a flat split lightbox, without nested cards.

## FAQ

1. Questions are grouped by planning, technical production and event logistics.
2. A pinned numeric index tracks the active group.
3. Answers expand as luminous horizontal bands with restrained height animation.
4. Unresolved questions flow directly into contact.

## Contact

1. One direct question precedes the form.
2. Event type, date, location and desired effects form the first compact row.
3. Contact details remain visible; extra production details stay collapsed.
4. The submission state replaces the form in place.

## Footer

1. A blue horizon line enters with scroll.
2. The FireArtRo wordmark fills the width without becoming a separate card.
3. Contact, social and legal links remain sparse and keyboard accessible.
