# Night Runway - Decisions

## Locked Direction

- **Director / film grammar:** Joseph Kosinski, `TRON: Legacy`.
- **Visual premise:** a premium night-sky launch corridor built from darkness, white type, electric-blue light and real FireArtRo footage.
- **Preserved brand elements:** existing hero background video sources, logo, desktop/mobile header behavior and social dock destinations.
- **Rebuilt surface:** every other page composition, card silhouette, button shape, spacing system, content hierarchy, motion behavior and responsive layout. The redesign is explicitly structural and cosmetic; legacy component shapes and surface styling are not retained.

## Design Rules

- Surfaces use obsidian `#03050a`, carbon `#080c14`, paper white `#f5f7fb`, ice blue `#8dd3ff` and electric blue `#1677ff`.
- Blue light is structural, not decorative: borders, paths, focus states and scene depth.
- No generic glass cards, nested cards, rounded-pill overload, gradient blobs or repeated three-column marketing grids.
- Primary buttons use clipped/angled geometry; secondary actions use a restrained outlined rail treatment.
- One signature interaction per page. Motion supports hierarchy and never hides conversion actions on first paint.
- Sora carries display moments; Inter carries compact controls and body copy.
- GSAP may provide structural transitions; the landing service stage remains video-led without a Three.js overlay.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`, keyboard focus, 44px targets, semantic landmarks and WCAG AA contrast are mandatory.

## Page Signatures

- **Landing:** video hero followed by a scroll-driven electric runway and full-bleed service chapters.
- **Packages:** vertical editorial index beside one active package poster.
- **Gallery:** compact projection table with a numbered filter rail and filmstrip.
- **FAQ:** signal-index navigation with expanding answer bands.
- **Contact:** final-ignition brief builder with a compact, confidence-led form.
- **Footer:** oversized wordmark, horizon line and minimal operational links.

## Copy Rules

- Lead with outcome, atmosphere and operational confidence.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives, invented awards, client counts or safety claims.
- CTAs use explicit actions: `Planifica spectacolul`, `Vezi proiectul`, `Compara pachetele`, `Trimite brief-ul`.
- Romanian copy keeps diacritics and short sentence rhythm.
