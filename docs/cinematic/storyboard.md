# FireArtRo Storyboard

## Site Grammar

- Shell: dark full-bleed stage with blue illumination.
- Navigation: restrained overlay that exits on downward scroll.
- Framing: large visual field, compact information, strong vertical sequencing.
- Density: spectacle, information, pause, payoff.
- Motion: one primary scroll-controlled gesture per major scene.

## Home Arc

1. Hero: video-first emotional promise.
2. Prologue: short cinematic build-up.
3. Capacity: compact proof.
4. Showcase: real event visuals.
5. Safety: operational confidence.
6. Process: centered production timeline.
7. Offer journey: vertical rail drives one aligned image/text scene.
8. Media preview: compact horizontal cinematic slider.
9. Trust and partners: verified placeholders with staggered reveal.
10. Final CTA: expanded blue night field.
11. Footer: editorial end credits.

## Interior Scenes

- Gallery: curved draggable media orbit.
- Packages: compact filterable catalogue.
- Contact: dedicated brief scene with reduced friction.
