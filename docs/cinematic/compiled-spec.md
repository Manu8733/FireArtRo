# FireArtRo Compiled Spec

## Motion System

- Heavy interaction: offer journey scroll choreography.
- Showy reveal 1: gallery orbit.
- Showy reveal 2: partner stagger rail.
- Other motion remains subordinate.
- Reduced motion: transitions disabled and content remains fully available.

## Entrance Map

- Hero: video reveal.
- Prologue: scene dissolve and controlled crop.
- Proof: focus scale.
- Showcase: pinned scene transition.
- Process: vertical progress and finale burst.
- Offers: clip aperture with image zoom.
- Gallery preview: horizontal snap.
- Partners: alternating stagger.
- CTA: ambient field.

## External Library Decision

- Core motion: scroll narrative and media orbit.
- Native stack: Framer Motion and CSS are sufficient.
- External libraries: none.

## Typography

- Sora: primary headings and controls.
- Inter: body and operational content.
- Bebas Neue: editorial display accents only; never used for paragraphs or form labels.

## Responsive

- Desktop/laptop: scroll choreography and full visual alignment.
- Mobile: same offer choreography with compact horizontal rail.
- Apple/WebKit: transforms remain enabled; blur-heavy effects are avoided and video preload is limited.
