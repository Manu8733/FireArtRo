# FireArtRo Product Marketing Context

## Product

FireArtRo plans and produces drone shows, professional fireworks, synchronized
drone and fireworks shows, cold sparks, and special effects for events in
Romania.

## Primary Audiences

- Couples and private-event organizers
- Corporate event and product-launch teams
- Festival, city-event, and venue organizers
- Agencies looking for a technical production partner

## Primary Conversion

The main action is a qualified quote request. The visitor should understand the
available formats, see real visual proof, trust the production process, and
reach `/contact` with enough context for an initial evaluation.

## Voice And Visual Direction

- Direct, modern, human, technically credible
- Premium through composition and restraint, not luxury language
- Compact headings and short supporting copy
- Blue-black visual system with light blue as the main accent and limited violet
- Real event imagery and cinematic motion, without sci-fi interface decoration

## Content Guardrails

- Do not invent clients, reviews, partners, certifications, awards, prices, or
  production results.
- Use "approximately 150 drones"; never imply a larger verified fleet.
- Treat placeholder testimonials and partners as unpublished content.
- Explain safety, permits, logistics, and feasibility without guarantees that
  depend on location, weather, or authorities.
- Keep one clear action per section and prefer "Solicita oferta" for the primary
  conversion.
